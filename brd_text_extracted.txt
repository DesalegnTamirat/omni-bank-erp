Part 1 Performance Management System 
1. Introduction 
In today’s competitive and rapidly changing banking environment, effective performance management is 
essential to achieve  strategic goals, improve service delivery, strengthen accountability, and support 
sustainable growth. The Bank requires a modern and integrated ERP Performance Management System 
(PMS) Module that ensures consistent planning, monitoring, evaluation, and employee development across 
all levels. 
 
Existing manual or fragmented processes often result in delays, inconsistent evaluations, limited 
transparency, and weak alignment between employee goals and organizational objectives. To address 
these challenges, the Bank requires a centralized digital solution that automates performance processes, 
improves collaboration between employees and supervisors, and provides reliable data for decision -
making. 
 
The enhanced ERP Performance Management System Module (PMS) shall  manage the full performance 
lifecycle, including goal setting, KPI management, continuous feedback, appraisals, competency 
assessments, and development planning, analytics, and reward linkage. It shall also integrate with existing 
enterprise systems while meeting high standards of security, scalability, flexibility, and user accessibility. 
 
This document outlines the functional, technical, and governance requirements for implementing a future-
ready ERP Performance Management System (PMS) Module that will e nhance productivity, talent 
management, and the Bank's long-term success. 
1.1 Current Challenges 
The Bank's current ERP Performance Management System Module environment faces several challenges, 
including: 
• Weak alignment and cascading of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). 
• Manual and inefficient performance management processes. 
• Limited system integration and automation. 
• Low user adoption and engagement. 
• Insufficient analytics and reporting capabilities. 
• Limited tracking of employee development plans, competencies, and continuous feedback. 
• Difficulty ensuring consistency, transparency, and auditability of performance evaluations.

1.2 Operational Structure 
• Head Office (HO): Senior Leadership and central support units. 
• District Offices: District hubs overseeing branch operations. 
• Branch Network: Extensive nationwide presence across urban and rural areas. 
1.3 Organizational Hierarchy for PMS 
The ERP PMS module must fully align with the following hierarchy to enable seamless top-down cascading 
and bottom-up reporting: 
• Tier-I: Chief Executive Officer (CEO). 
• Tier-II-A: Senior Management Level (Chiefs). 
• Tier-II-B: Director Level. 
• Tier-II: District Director and Branch Manager Level. 
• Tier-III (Management): TOT, SAT, Senior Team, Team Leader, Division, Operatio n/Customer Service 
Manager, and Business Managers. 
• Tier-III (Staff): Individual staff level. 
2. Objectives and Expected Outcomes 
The enhanced and updated ERP Performance Management System (PMS) Module is expected to achieve 
the following strategic and operational objectives: 
2.1 Objectives 
• Implement a fully automated end-to-end PMS process across the Bank. 
• Enable continuous performance management beyond the traditional annual appraisal cycle. 
• Achieve Level 4 PMS maturity (Managed and Integrated). 
• Align employee performance with the Bank's strategic objectives. 
• Cascade Bank-wide strategic goals — such as deposit mobilization, customer base, Non-Performing 
Loan (NPL) reduction, and digital adoption — down to departmental, branch, team, and individual 
employee KPIs. 
• Enable competency-based performance planning, monitoring, and evaluation. 
• Provide real-time tracking of KPIs, goals, and overall performance progress. 
• Support fair, transparent, standardized, and auditable evaluation processes across all branches, 
districts, and head office. 
• Eliminate paper-based forms, manual spreadsheet tracking, and email-based approval workflows. 
• Strengthen talent retention and employee development by identifying high -potential employees, 
competency gaps, and training needs.

• Integrate performance outcomes with Succession Planning, Learning & Development (L&D), rewards, 
and recognition programs. 
• Enable data -driven decision -making for HR leadership and management through dashboards, 
analytics, and reporting tools. 
2.2 Expected Outcomes 
• Reduced Performance Review Cycle Time: Improve appraisal turnaround time from approximately 60 
days to 20 days or less. 
• Improved Completion Rate: Achieve 100% compliance with performance review timelines across all 
branches and business units. 
• Enhanced Data Integrity: Establish a single source of truth for all employee performance records and 
appraisal data. 
• Higher Employee Engagement: Improve engagement through continuous feedback, regular check -
ins, and clear visibility of career growth opportunities. 
• Stronger Strategic Execution: Ensure individual and team performance directly contributes to 
achieving Bank-wide goals. 
• Improved Fairness and Accountability: Standardized evaluation criteria and transparent processes 
across the organization. 
3. Scope 
The enhanced and updated ERP Performance Management System (PMS) Module shall include, but not be 
limited to, the following functions: 
• Manage the end-to-end employee performance management lifecycle. 
• Enable managers to assign, monitor, and track goals and objectives. 
• Support employee self-assessment and peer review processes. 
• Generate performance reports, dashboards, and analytics. 
• Support KPI-based and competency-based performance evaluations. 
• Facilitate continuous feedback, coaching, and performance discussions. 
• Integrate with Learning Management Systems (LMS) and other relevant internal systems. 
• Support workflow automation, approvals, and notifications. 
• Maintain secure records and audit trails for all performance activities. 
4. Business Rules 
The PMS Module shall operate under the following business rules:

• Applicability: The PMS applies to all permanent employees across Head Office and branch operations. 
• Single Source of Truth: All performance data shall originate exclusively from the PMS module and shall 
synchronize with HRIS/ERP, Payroll, and CBS without manual intervention. 
• Hierarchy-Driven Cascading: All goal cascading shall strictly follow the approved organizational 
hierarchy, from CEO (Tier-I) down to individual staff (Tier-III). 
• Balanced Scorecard & SMART Criteria: All objectives shall be defined using the Balanced Scorecard 
perspectives (Financial, Customer/Stakeholder, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth) and 
SMART criteria, weighted by strategic importance. 
• Evaluation Frequency: Performance evaluations shall be conducted semi-annually and Quarterly 
• KPI Composition: Each employee's Individual Performance Plan shall contain 5 –8 SMART KPIs with 
weights totaling 100%. 
• Eligibility-Based Appraisal: Individual performance appraisals shall apply only to p ermanent 
employees who have served at least three months during the evaluation period. 
• Segregation of Duties: No single user shall initiate, approve, and finalize the same performance 
transaction. 
• Immutability: Finalized performance scores and audit logs s hall be locked and non -editable after 
approval, and retained per compliance policy. 
• Configurability: Rating scales, weighting, KPI libraries, and competency frameworks shall be 
configurable to accommodate evolving Bank policy without custom development. 
• Grievance: Grievance appeals shall follow the defined 4-step escalation path (Supervisor → PPDD → 
Grievance Committee) with fixed working -day  at each step, and Committee decisions shall be final 
and binding. 
• Regulatory Compliance: All processes shall comply  with Ethiopian Labor Law and applicable Bank 
policy.

Part 2 Employee Development System 
1. Introduction 
The Employee Development System (EDS) is an integrated module of Bunna  Bank S.C.'s ERP system that 
automates and manages the end -to-end employee learning and development lifecycle. The system 
supports Training Needs Assessment (TNA), curriculum and course management, annual learning 
planning, training delivery, evaluation, sponsorship, learning partnerships, and reporting. 
The EDS integrates with the Bank's HRIS, Performance Management System (PMS), Learning Management 
System (LMS), Succession Planning, Career Development, Payroll, Finance, and other ERP modules to 
provide a centralized, secure, and auditable platform for employee development. 
2. Objectives 
The Employee Development System aims to: 
 Digitize and standardize the end-to-end Learning and Development (L&D) process.  
 Align employee development with competency requirements, performance, and business 
objectives.  
 Automate workflows, approvals, notifications, and audit trails.  
 Improve planning, delivery, monitoring, and evaluation of learning programs.  
 Provide integrated reporting, dashboards, and analytics for informed decision-making.  
 Maintain a centralized employee learning history and development record. 
2. Purpose 
The purpose of the Employee Development System is to provide a centralized and automated platform for 
planning, managing, delivering, monitoring, and evaluating employee learning and development activities. 
The system ensures that all learning interventions are competency -based, properly governed, fully 
traceable, and integrated with the Bank's ERP.

6. Business Rules 
The EDS shall operate under the following overarching business rules:

• TNA-Driven: All classroom training data shall originate from Training Needs Analysis (TNA) entries or 
a documented ad-hoc exception with justification. 
• Single Source of Truth: Employee, job role, and organizational data shall be sourced exclusively from 
Employee Master Data (HRIS); no duplicate manual entry shall be permitted in EDS. 
• Segregation of Duties: No single user shall both nominate/approve a part icipant and record their 
evaluation score for the same session. 
• Role-Based Access: System access shall be restricted based on defined roles (Employee, Manager, 
Trainer, L&D Admin, Executive). 
• Audit Trail: Every nomination, approval, attendance record, and evaluation score shall be recorded 
with Timestamp, User ID, and prior/new value where applicable. 
• Certification Gating: Certificate issuance shall be gated by a configurable rule requiring attendance 
and, where applicable, a passing Level 2 assessment score. 
• LMS Routing: All classroom training requiring e-learning delivery shall be routed to the LMS rather than 
duplicated within EDS. 
• Immutability: Audit logs and evaluation records shall be non -editable and retained per Bank 
compliance policy. 
7. Module: Employee Development System (EDS) - Classroom Training Management 
7.1 Business Rules 
The EDS module shall apply the following module-specific business rules: 
• Delivery Mode Classification: A training need shall be classified as Classroom, E-Learning, or Blended 
at the point of TNA consolidation; only Classroom and the classroom component of Blended needs 
shall be actioned within EDS. 
• Capacity Control: Nominations shall not exceed the configured maximum capacity of a session; 
excess nominations shall be waitlisted. 
• Approval Hierarchy: Nomination and approval shall follow a configurable multi -level workflow (Line 
Manager to L&D, with Budget/HR). 
• Ad-Hoc Exception Handling: Ad-hoc nominations outside the approved TNA register shall require 
documented justification and L&D approval.

Part 5 Competency Management System 
 
1. Introduction 
1.1 Purpose 
This business Requirements defines the business and system requirements for the implementation of 
Bunna Bank's integrated Competency Management, Career Path, Succession Planning, and Learning & 
Development System  within the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Automated  Performance 
Management System (APMS), Employee Development System (EDS), and Learning Management System 
(LMS). 
The system will automate competency-based talent management processes and provide a single platform 
for managing competencies, career development, succession planning, learning, and workforce capability 
development across the Bank. 
1.2 Scope 
This document covers the functional and non -functional requirements for an integrated solution 
supporting: 
 Competency Framework and Dictionary Management  
 Competency Mapping and Assessment  
 Competency Gap Analysis  
 Career Path and Career Development Management  
 Succession Planning and Critical Position Management  
 Talent Assessment and Readiness Management  
 Individual Development Planning (IDP/ICDP)  
 Learning and Development Planning

 Continuous Learning Credit (CLC) Management

The solution shall integrate with the Bank's HRIS, Performance Management System (PMS/APMS), Employee

1.3 Business Context 
Bunna Bank's Integrated Competency Framework establishes a common foundation for competency-based 
people management by defining the knowledge, skills, behaviors, and leadership capabilities required 
across all job families and organizational levels. Built on three competency pillars —Core, Leadership, and 
Technical—the framework supports consistent recruitment, performance management, learning and 
development, career progression, succession planning, and workforce planning. 
To operationalize this framework, the Bank requires an integrated digital solution that automates 
competency assessment, identifies capability gaps, supports structured career development, strengthens 
succession planning, recommends targeted learning interventions, and provides real -time analytics for 
strategic talent de cisions. The system will replace fragmented manual processes with standardized 
workflows, ensuring transparency, governance, and alignment between employee capability development 
and the Bank's strategic objectives.

 Implement a standardized competency-based talent management framework across the Bank.  
 Establish and maintain Core, Leadership, and Technical competency frameworks for all job families 
and career levels.  
 Assess employee  competencies and identify competency gaps at individual, team, and 
organizational levels.
