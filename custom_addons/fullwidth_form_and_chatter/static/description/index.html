App Overview

The FullWidth Form & Chatter app enhances your Odoo interface by expanding form views to full width and optimizing the chatter section. With this app, you'll have more space to work with forms and communications, making your user experience more efficient and streamlined.

Full Width Form Views

Makes form views full width, giving users more space for entering and viewing data.

Chatter at Bottom

Moves the chatter section down and makes it full width for better readability.

Better Focus

Enhances the UI for better focus on both form fields and communication threads.

Decluttered UI

Simplifies the interface and reduces visual noise in busy form-based processes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about installation, configuration, and using the app. If you don't see your question here, feel free to reach out - we're happy to help!

What does the FullWidth Form & Chatter app do?
It expands Odoo form views to use the full screen width and moves the chatter section to the bottom, making the layout more spacious and easier to navigate.
Does it support all Odoo models and views?
Yes. The layout enhancement works on all standard and custom form views where the chatter is present.
Is any technical configuration required?
No. The module works out-of-the-box after installation. No coding or manual XML modifications are needed.
Will this affect the functionality of the chatter?
No. The chatter remains fully functional - including sending messages, logging notes, and scheduling activities.
Does this module affect kanban, tree, or other views?
No. This module only modifies the layout of form views. Other view types like list, kanban, calendar, etc., are untouched.
Can I disable it on specific models?
Currently, it applies globally to all form views with chatter. If you need model-level control, please contact us for a customized version.
Is it compatible with Odoo Enterprise?
Yes, the app is fully compatible with both Odoo Community and Enterprise editions.
