# End-to-End User Guide: Bunna Bank Succession Management

This guide walks you through the entire Succession Management lifecycle. It is designed for PPDD Officers and SPMC Members to ensure business continuity by identifying, tracking, and developing the next generation of bank leaders.

---

## Phase 1: Identifying Critical Positions
**General Description:** Not every job needs a succession plan. This step identifies the most crucial roles in the bank—positions where a sudden vacancy would cause severe operational or financial damage.

**How to do it:**
1. Navigate to **Succession Pipeline ➔ Critical Positions**.
2. Click **New**.
3. Select the target **Job Position**.
4. Set the **Impact of Vacancy** and **Retention Risk**. The system will auto-calculate a Criticality Score.
5. Click **Submit to SPMC**, and the SPMC will approve the position as "Critical".

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> You identify the role of **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)**. You set the Impact to "High" and Retention Risk to "Medium". The SPMC approves it. The bank now officially recognizes the CTO role as a critical risk area that needs a backup plan.

---

## Phase 2: Building Talent Pools (Optional)
**General Description:** Before nominating a specific person for a specific job, you can group high-potential employees into a general "Talent Pool." This helps PPDD keep track of rising stars.

**How to do it:**
1. Navigate to **Succession Pipeline ➔ Talent Pools**.
2. Click **New** and name the pool.
3. Add employees based on their recent Performance (PMS) and Potential (9-Box) scores.

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> You create a Talent Pool called **"Future IT Leaders"** and add 5 Senior IT Managers into it, including an employee named **Abebe Negash**.

---

## Phase 3: Candidate Nomination & Assessment
**General Description:** This is where you officially link an employee to a Critical Position and assess if they are ready to take over tomorrow, or if they need more training.

**How to do it:**
1. Navigate to **Succession Pipeline ➔ Succession Pipeline**.
2. Click **New**.
3. Select the **Employee** and the **Target Critical Position**.
4. Click **Auto Match**. The system compares the competencies required by the Job against the Employee's actual skills.
5. Click **Compute Gap Analysis**. The system lists exactly what skills the employee is missing.
6. Review the system-calculated **Readiness Score** (e.g., "Ready in 1-2 Years").
7. Click **Submit to SPMC**.

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> You nominate **Abebe Negash** to be the successor for the **CTO** position. You click Auto-Match, and the system shows Abebe is strong in "Technical Knowledge" but lacks "Executive Strategic Planning". The system gives him a readiness score of "Ready in 1-2 Years". 

---

## Phase 4: Executive Governance (SPMC Approval)
**General Description:** Nominations must be reviewed by senior leadership. The SPMC acts as the final gatekeeper to ensure the right people are chosen.

**How to do it:**
1. An SPMC Member logs in and opens the candidate record.
2. They review the candidate's Gap Analysis, PMS scores, and 9-Box placement.
3. They click **SPMC Approve**.

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> The SPMC committee reviews Abebe's file. They agree he is the right fit for the future CTO role and click **SPMC Approve**. Because he is "Ready in 1-2 Years" (not Ready Now), the system knows he needs a development plan.

---

## Phase 5: Successor Development Plan (IDP)
**General Description:** This is the execution phase. PPDD creates targeted activities to close the specific competency gaps identified in Phase 3. 

**How to do it:**
1. Navigate to **Successor Development ➔ Successor Development Plans (IDP)**.
2. Click **New** and select the Candidate.
3. In the **Development Activities** tab, click *Add a line*.
4. Fill in the **Activity Type**, **Description**, **Start/Target Dates**, and link it to the specific competency gap.
5. Click **Activate Plan**.
6. Over the coming months, PPDD updates the **Progress (%)** and changes the Status to **In Progress** ➔ **Completed**.
7. When all activities are 100% done, click **Mark Completed** at the top of the plan.

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> PPDD creates an IDP for Abebe. They add an activity: *"Executive Fintech Leadership Course"* to close his "Strategic Planning" gap. It starts Oct 20th and ends Dec 10th. Once Abebe finishes the course, PPDD marks it 100% Complete. Abebe is now fully trained and officially **"Ready Now"**.

---

## Phase 6: The End Goal - Deployment!
**General Description:** The ultimate conclusion of the succession lifecycle. The current executive leaves, and the successor is formally placed into the role.

**How to do it:**
1. Open the **Succession Candidate** profile.
2. Click the **Initiate Deployment** button.
3. The candidate's status changes to "Deployed", finalizing their journey from nominee to the actual seat holder.

> **📖 Example Scenario:** 
> Two years later, the current CTO retires. PPDD goes into Abebe's succession profile and clicks **Initiate Deployment**. Abebe officially becomes the new CTO of Bunna Bank. The succession plan was a complete success!

---

## Phase 7: Reports & Analytics (Management Dashboards)
**General Description:** Throughout the year, PPDD and Executives use reports to view the overall health and risk levels of the entire organization at a glance.

**Key Reports:**
* **Bench Strength Analysis:** Found under *Reports & Analytics*. It shows a list of all critical positions and how many successors are "Ready Now" vs "Ready in 1-2 Years." 
  * *Example:* PPDD uses this to see that the Finance department has zero "Ready Now" candidates, prompting them to focus recruitment there.
* **9-Box Talent Grid:** Found under *Succession Pipeline*. A visual chart plotting Performance vs. Potential.
  * *Example:* Executives look at the top-right box ("Star Performers") to see if those employees have been assigned to critical positions yet.
* **Succession Dashboard:** The landing page of the module. It highlights "Positions at Risk" (critical roles with no approved successors).
